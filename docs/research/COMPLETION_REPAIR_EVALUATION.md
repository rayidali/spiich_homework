## Predeclared failure and accounting policy

Unlike the closed predecessor, a settled isolated HTTP/model/schema failure ends that cell and is scored as a failure; execution may continue to the next distinct scheduled cell. Generic HTTP 400/`INVALID_ARGUMENT`, bounded malformed responses and missing usage do not themselves terminate the entire comparison. Unknown usage retains the **entire original reservation**, even after the cell closes. Cells are never repeated or replaced. Existing product-internal schema repair remains part of the route: at most two provider attempts for composable and three for either candidate. A transport-failed cell cannot obtain another attempt.

Stop the entire cohort on authentication, permission, billing/precondition, quota/rate or model-availability errors; budget or attempt exhaustion; source/book changes; credential leakage; reservation overflow; persistence/accounting corruption; concurrent requests; or unresolved network/abort/in-flight state. HTTP 401, 402, 403, 404 and 429 and recognized equivalent provider statuses are global stops. A stopped phase is terminal, with every completed cell and reservation preserved. There is no reset, retry, replacement model, automatic refill or holdout dispatch after incomplete development.

A phase is operationally complete only with every scheduled cell recorded, unchanged full book fingerprints and source, and a closed ledger with no pending request, active trial or global halt. Closed unknown reservations are allowed and reconciled as `knownUsd + unknownReservedUsd = chargedUsd`. All twelve development cells and their immutable artifact are required before holdout at the exact same source and private permit. Development correctness is reported; operational completion does not turn failed cells into passing answers.
