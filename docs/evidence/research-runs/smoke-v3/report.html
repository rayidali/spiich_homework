Methods and limits

  1. All inputs are synthetic. References are independently implemented decimal fixture calculations, not audited business records. No independent human adjudication is claimed.
  2. The primary score measures structured task success against frozen facts, scope, evidence IDs, proposal lines and explicit assumption/clarification tags. Natural-language summary faithfulness and usefulness still need human review.
  3. Grounding scores measure source provenance: required source IDs, actual result-value bindings and deterministic calculations. They do not establish full semantic entailment of every citation. Supplemental claims without an independent reference or exact trusted-result binding are penalized as unassessed.
  4. Automated scores are out of 90. The 10-point human clarity dimension and the combined 100-point score are unavailable; they are not imputed by another model.
  5. Provider/tool errors count as failed end-to-end episodes. Conditional quality excludes status:error episodes and includes intentional blocks; it is not a replacement denominator.
  6. Confidence intervals resample scenario groups within families. Paraphrases and repeat runs are correlated and stay together. Small synthetic samples do not establish real-world accuracy or safety.
  7. All displayed 95% bootstrap intervals are unadjusted for the nine predeclared component contrasts and additional descriptive contrasts. They are exploratory effect estimates, not simultaneous confidence guarantees or claims of statistical significance. Two-group pilot and five-group-per-family held-out intervals may have poor coverage; zero-width empirical intervals do not establish certainty.
  8. Reported monetary amounts are paid-equivalent estimates from returned usage and configured rates, not invoices. Unreported usage remains a separately identified conservative reservation; indexing/setup is separate.
  9. Managed File Search uses the Interactions API to identify source citations. Its citation order is not a raw retrieval rank or exposed passage list. Authorized source IDs are mapped to the shared canonical documents before answer generation. This is managed source selection, not a like-for-like chunk retriever; raw-passage recall, MRR and nDCG are unavailable unless independently exposed.
  10. Jev/LLM reviewers see supplied evidence, not ground truth, and can share generator errors. Their approval cannot override deterministic validation or database permissions.
  11. The Wren route evaluates the pinned OSS semantic engine with the shared planner and approved views. It does not measure the complete commercial Wren product.
  12. An incomplete route has no full-study conclusion. Missing episodes are reported separately, never silently removed or treated as free successful requests.
  13. Episode completeness and snapshot integrity are separate gates. Changed or unverified before/after data makes this report ineligible for comparative selection even if every episode is present. Individual routes with hard-gate violations remain disqualified.
  14. Queue-transport wall latency includes local dispatch delays and is not production request latency. Provider durations are measured separately.
  15. All study generators normalized to Gemini3.5FlashLite; R0 is an architecture reproduction. Same-model critic errors may correlate.
