## The distinction behind the office-supplies failure

There are two different jobs:

1. **Do the arithmetic correctly.** If the chosen records contain $100 and $50 of supplies, their total is $150.
2. **Choose the right records and finish the question.** An exact total of every expense still fails a question asking only about supplies. Two correct monthly totals still fail a request that also asks for their difference.

Daybook has substantial evidence for the first job. The second remains the central reliability gap. A model's statement that its work is complete is not independent proof. The new evaluator therefore starts from the original question's requirements, checks meaning as well as dollars, and rejects a correct answer buried under an unrelated headline.

## What the Jev result actually means

Both selectors chose semantically acceptable categories in **33/33 measured category inputs**. When the score also required following the taught business preference, Gemini scored **33/33** and Jev **31/33**. Jev's two misses came from the same scenario at two history states. These are not 33 independent businesses or two independent failed scenarios.

The held-out design contains eighteen scenario sequences, each observed at three hypothetical history states: 54 inputs per selector. Of those, 42 allow a model decision; twelve are local-gate outcomes and must not be counted as model successes. The 33 category inputs come from eleven sequences. Across only five teaching sequences, eventual preference adoption went from 2/5 to 5/5 for Gemini and 2/5 to 4/5 for Jev. This shows use of supplied examples in this small sample, not training the model's weights or measuring longitudinal users. [Frozen results](evidence/category-runs/completed-v3/holdout/report.md), [protocol](CATEGORIZATION_PROTOCOL.md), [decision](evidence/category-runs/completed-v3/release-decision.json).

The source audit found that the current narrow selector uses its own provider adapters. Its selector, service and correction-memory code are unchanged from the measured implementation; later financial-chat changes do not pass through that adapter. Of eighteen evaluation-bound files, seventeen are identical. The remaining change rejects year 0000 and leaves all measured inputs unchanged. Pure reconstruction reproduced all 72 input/snapshot pairs across the 216 frozen plan slots, and all 228 exported evidence files matched their recorded hashes. This supports retaining the historical narrow-selector conclusion without spending on an unnecessary rerun. It does not give the changed whole-entry system that score. [Source/input audit receipt](evidence/goal-category-relevance-audit.json).

For the user-facing learning path, imagine the AI suggests Office supplies and you choose Printing instead. **Posting the reviewed entry** stores that correction together with its accounting transaction. Merely saving a draft does not teach. A later suggestion can receive the valid example; reversing, superseding or forgetting it removes its teaching effect. The database/browser tests exercise that path with controlled generation. The selector study supplies prepared history snapshots, so the combination is not a measured live end-to-end learning rate. ## What follows

The immediate repair work is bounded private error diagnostics and investigation of the complete route’s larger declaration grammar. The failed cohort is closed; its holdout cannot proceed. Any revised experiment must declare its changed source and preserve this failure, its unknown reservation and the untouched held-out isolation. A route that refuses answerable questions does not receive a pass for being cautious. No qualifying result means no automatic promotion.

If a candidate qualifies, source-specific staged/hosted acceptance is still required before promotion. The new comparison does not prove rendered usability, production scale, arbitrary code generation or whole-entry learning accuracy. Those remaining requirements must stay visible rather than being silently absorbed into a chat score. Existing categorization evidence remains useful within its stated scope; no closed experiment may be reopened or relabeled.
