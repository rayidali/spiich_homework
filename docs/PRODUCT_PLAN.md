Started 2026-10-31 under the user's active goal to fix the audited gaps, implement missing product behavior and compare alternatives before choosing responsibilities. This is a new product iteration; the completed architecture study and its closed spending ledger remain unchanged.

## Completion rule

Every original assignment requirement must have a usable workflow and evidence covering its success and failure paths. Passing mocked AI responses is not evidence of provider accuracy. A new category comparison must evaluate the actual implemented selectors on identical inputs and preserve failures. No million-user or perfect-accuracy claim is a completion criterion.

The goal stays active until the work below is implemented, tested, delivered and reflected in the checkpoint. This plan does not narrow the original assignment; it turns the audit into explicit checks.

## Immediate design decisions

- Error references are tracking numbers, not saved copies of private conversations. Start with safe structured runtime logs. Add Sentry or another service only if the evidence shows the existing runtime-log workflow is inadequate; the user offered access if needed.
- A reviewed posting is the learning boundary. Saving a draft alone does not teach the model. The server stores its own structured proposal and validates the entry binding; arbitrary client-supplied examples are not trusted.
- Posting and recording a meaningful correction are one database transaction. The interface must not claim that a choice was remembered after a separate hidden failure.
- Automatically resolved anomaly alerts retain their history and reason. They are not silently deleted or labeled as user-dismissed.
- Keep numerical accounting in exact deterministic code. Models propose accounts/meaning and ask questions; they do not bypass posting validation or user review.

## Comparison discipline and spending

Use a new versioned categorization evaluation; do not edit or reuse the old holdout as new evidence. Current rent/party failures are development regressions. Compare identical validated transaction facts and correction history. Keep extraction/retrieval fixed for the selector comparison and separately measure the real product's full conversation flow.

Plan development and held-out scenario groups before execution. Measure category, counteraccount/direction, complete-entry validity, appropriate clarification, coverage, learning gains and harmful generalization separately. Count provider failures and abstentions; preserve all attempts. Pick thresholds only on development, report uncertainty and avoid declaring a winner from a small numerical difference.

Verify current provider API/pricing and declare a small persistent additional cost/request ceiling before paid execution. Use the existing authorized local keys, make no purchase or subscription change, and preserve unknown-usage reservations. The earlier study's closed budget and artifacts must not be reset. Code, fixtures and offline checks proceed before any new paid evaluation.

## Delivery sequence

1. Integrate and verify observability, memory and anomaly repairs.
2. Implement the category selectors and focused evaluation using the repaired memory contract.
3. Test, compare and choose product responsibilities from evidence; address failures and re-evaluate only under an explicitly versioned change.
4. Complete scheduler, currency, reports, import/create and browser acceptance; improve confusing interactions found during those checks.
5. Run final CI/Compose and protected hosted acceptance, publish the implementation, update the explanatory report and refresh the verified submission archive.
